# CommentCard

One comment of a list: where it is, who wrote it and when, its text and
its replies.

<Canvas of={CommentCard.Default} />

## Variants

`is-open` outlines the comment open on the canvas; `is-resolved` fades a
settled one.

<Canvas of={CommentCard.Open} />
<Canvas of={CommentCard.Resolved} />

## Technical notes

The card is a `role="button"` that reacts to Enter and Space. Two slots
take elements: `avatar` and `anchor` (a chip with what the comment points
at). The text of the comment goes in `children`. The caller translates
`location`, `time` and `replies`.

```clj
[:> comment-card* {:location "#1 - Board"
                   :author (:fullname owner)
                   :time (ct/timeago (:modified-at thread))
                   :replies (tr "workspace.review.comments.no-replies")
                   :avatar (mf/html [:> avatar* {:profile owner :variant "M"}])
                   :anchor (mf/html [:> layer-chip* {:label "Card form"}])
                   :on-click on-open}
 (:content thread)]
```
