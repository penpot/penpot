# ChangeRow

One entry of a change list: a coloured bar for its status, an icon, a
title over a one-line summary, and a slot at each end.

<Canvas of={ChangeRow.Default} />

## Variants

`end` takes a tag or a count.

<Canvas of={ChangeRow.WithTag} />

`start` takes a checkbox or a chevron. `is-muted` dims a row that is
left out.

<Canvas of={ChangeRow.WithCheckbox} />

## Technical notes

The row acts as a button (`role="button"`, Enter and Space call
`on-click`). Controls in the slots must stop the click from reaching
the row.

```clj
[:> change-row* {:title (:label item)
                 :subtitle summary
                 :icon i/board
                 :tone "info"
                 :is-selected selected?
                 :end (mf/html [:> tag* {:variant "pill"} "Added"])
                 :on-click on-select}]
```
