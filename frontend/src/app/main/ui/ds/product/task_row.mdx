# TaskRow

One task of a list: its title over its state and the layer it points at.

<Canvas of={TaskRow.Default} />

## Variants

`is-selected` outlines the task open on the canvas, in the review colour
with `is-review`. `review` shows the name of the design review it belongs to.

<Canvas of={TaskRow.Selected} />
<Canvas of={TaskRow.InAReview} />

`is-layer-lost` marks a task whose layer was deleted: the chip of the
layer turns orange and shows a warning.

<Canvas of={TaskRow.LostLayer} />

## Technical notes

The row is a `role="button"` that reacts to Enter and Space while the focus
is on the row itself. Two slots
take elements: `assignee` (an avatar, at the end of the second line)
and `actions` (a menu trigger, at the end of the title). A click inside
a slot must stop its propagation when it should not open the task.

```clj
[:> task-row* {:title (:title task)
               :status (:status task)
               :status-label (tr "workspace.tasks.status.open")
               :layer "Card form"
               :assignee (mf/html [:> avatar* {:profile assignee :variant "S"}])
               :on-click on-open}]
```
