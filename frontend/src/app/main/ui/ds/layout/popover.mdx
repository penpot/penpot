# Popover

A panel that floats over the page while it is open, for content a
[Menu](?path=/docs/layout-menu--docs) cannot hold: rows with a status,
sections, a group that folds. It closes on a click out of it or on
**Escape**, and gives the focus back to where it was.

## Example

<Canvas of={Popover.Default} />

## Usage

```clj
[:> popover* {:is-open open?
              :on-close on-close
              :container trigger-ref}
 [:div {:class (stl/css :panel)} ...]]
```

- `is-open` shows the panel; `on-close` is called when it should close.
- `container` (optional) is a ref to an element whose clicks do not close
  it either, such as the bar that holds the trigger.
- A click on an element with a `data-no-close` property does not close it.

## Technical notes

The popover only behaves: it renders its children in place, with no box
of its own, and places and draws nothing. The caller positions and
styles the panel, so moving a panel to it changes nothing on screen.

It starts listening on the next tick, so the click that opened it does
not close it. On macOS the second event of a ctrl+click is ignored.
